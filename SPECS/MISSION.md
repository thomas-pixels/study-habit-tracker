# MISSION

## What we are building

A **Study Habit Tracker** — a simple, friendly website where students log
their study sessions and see their progress over time. It is built by
students, for students, and stays easy to read and learn from.

## Who it is for

Students who want to study more consistently but don't have a simple way to
see how much, how often, and on what subjects they study. The student opening
this app is a beginner just like us — they don't need a complicated tool,
just an honest, clear picture of their studying.

## The problem it solves

It is hard to build a study habit when you don't know what you actually do.
This app gives each student a clear, honest picture of their studying — how
much, how often, and on what subjects — so they can build confidence, stay
consistent, and improve over time. The goal is not to judge, but to help the
student understand their own habits.

## What a student can do

- **Log a study session:** pick a **subject**, enter **how long** they studied
  (in minutes), and add a **short note** about what they did.
- **See a last-7-days strip:** a simple row of boxes for the last 7 days,
  showing which days they studied and which they didn't.
- **See a streak counter:** showing how many days in a row they studied.
- **See total hours per subject:** to understand where their study time goes.

## What success looks like

A student uses the app after each study session and can see, at a glance, a
true picture of how consistent they have been. Over time they form a steady
habit: they study more often, spread their time sensibly across subjects, and
feel confident because they can see real progress.

## Values (non-negotiable)

- **Simple and approachable.** The app is easy to use right after a study
  session. No confusing features, no clutter, no jargon.
- **Honest data.** The app shows the student exactly what they logged. It
  does not guess, judge, or fill in information for them.
- **Inclusive for beginners.** Built by students, for students — the code
  stays clear, small, and easy to read and learn from.
- **Helpful, not punishing.** The app supports the student. It never shames
  them for a missed day; it simply shows the truth and encourages them to
  keep going.

## Design principles

- Work in small steps — deliver one working piece before moving to the next.
- Keep everything as simple as possible; the obvious solution beats a clever
  one.
- The whole app should make sense to a beginner reading it for the first time.

## Explicitly out of scope

- No user accounts or login system.
- No mobile app — just a website that works on phones.
- No reminders, notifications, social features, or leaderboards.
- No gamification, points, badges, or rankings.
